Submit an Incident Report

Provide factual information regarding suspected immigration offenses, human trafficking, smuggling, or fraud. You may choose 100% anonymous submission.

MANDATORY NOTICE: 18 U.S.C. § 1001 STATUTORY COMPLIANCE

Knowingly submitting false, fictitious, or malicious reports to federal law enforcement is a federal crime punishable by fines and up to 5 years imprisonment. Ensure all information is truthful to the best of your knowledge.

Official Incident Reporting Form

Complete the 4 steps below. You will receive an official tracking reference code upon submission.

1 Category & Location
2 Suspect & Activity
3 Evidence & Identity
4 Review & Affirm

Step 1: Select Violation Category & Location

Specify the primary offense category and the physical location where the suspected activity was observed.

Step 2: Suspect & Activity Details

Provide all known details regarding suspects, employers, organizations, or vehicles involved.

Step 3: Evidence Upload & Anonymity Preferences

Attach supporting evidence and select whether you wish to remain 100% anonymous or provide confidential whistleblower contact details.

When active, no identifying information, IP address, or digital signatures are linked to your report. You will receive an anonymous case reference code for tracking.

Whistleblower / Tipster Contact Details (Confidential)

Your identity is protected under law enforcement confidential informant guidelines. Only authorized federal investigators may contact you for clarifying details.
