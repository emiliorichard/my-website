Primary Enforcement Areas

Core Reporting Categories

To ensure swift action by the appropriate federal taskforce, select the category that most accurately describes the suspected illegal activity.

Immigration Violation

Unlawful presence, unauthorized employment schemes, willful failure to depart following a final order of removal, or evasion of federal immigration inspection.

Common Warning Signs & Indicators:
  • Workplaces systematically hiring unauthorized labor under the table
  • Individuals knowingly overstaying temporary visas or work authorizations
  • Absconders evading ICE Enforcement and Removal Operations (ERO)
  • Use of fraudulent Social Security numbers to gain employment
Report Immigration Violation →

Human Trafficking

Severe forms of trafficking involving force, fraud, or coercion for labor exploitation or commercial sex. Victims are often held through debt bondage or terror.

Critical Warning Signs & Indicators:
  • Withholding passports, IDs, or immigration documents by employers
  • Restricted movement, constant surveillance, or barred from speaking alone
  • Coerced commercial sexual exploitation in illicit massage or residential brothels
  • Extreme debt bondage with deductions preventing workers from leaving
Report Human Trafficking → Crisis Hotline: 1-888-373-7888

Human Smuggling

Organized commercial transportation or harboring of individuals across international borders in violation of U.S. immigration laws, often under perilous conditions.

Common Warning Signs & Indicators:
  • Suspected "stash houses" with heavy nighttime foot traffic and barred windows
  • Hazardous transport in commercial box trucks, tractor-trailers, or false compartments
  • Organized "coyote" operations coordinating clandestine crossings
  • Armed guards or transport facilitators monitoring groups in transit
Report Human Smuggling →

Immigration Fraud

Deceptive schemes to unlawfully obtain immigration benefits, including counterfeit identity documents, marriage fraud, falsified asylum claims, and unauthorized legal consultants.

Common Warning Signs & Indicators:
  • Sham marriages arranged strictly for financial payment to secure green cards
  • Production, sale, or distribution of fake Permanent Resident ("Green") cards
  • Fraudulent immigration consultants ("notarios") promising guaranteed visas
  • Forged employer sponsorships or shell companies used for work visas

Procedural Transparency

How Reporting Works in Three Steps

Our reporting process is engineered for speed, privacy, and evidentiary integrity. Follow these three simple steps to submit an actionable tip.

1

Select Violation Category & Location

Choose the category that best matches your observation. Enter the exact physical address, business name, or geographic coordinates where the violation is occurring.

  • Specific commercial or residential address
  • Dates, recurring times, and frequency
  • Company or employer details if applicable
2

Provide Details & Attach Evidence

Provide descriptions of suspects, vehicles, or aliases. You can securely upload photos, fraudulent documents, or records. Choose between 100% anonymous or confidential identified reporting.

  • Physical descriptions & license plates
  • Secure multi-file document / image upload
  • Zero personal data required if anonymous
3

Official Federal Review & Case Routing

Your submission is encrypted and immediately transmitted to federal investigators (ICE HSI, ERO, or USCIS). You receive a unique Case Reference ID for your records.

  • Instant unique Case Tracking ID issued
  • Triage by Homeland Security intelligence analysts
  • No public disclosure of informant data

Commitment to Fair, Objective, and Constitutional Reporting

Reports must be grounded in specific, verifiable, and observable facts. ICE REPORT strictly opposes racial profiling, discrimination, and unsubstantiated bias. Allegations based solely on race, ethnicity, religion, or spoken language are discarded and not forwarded to law enforcement agencies.
