Privacy & Security Statement

Detailed information on our zero-compromise encryption architecture, anonymous reporting guarantees, PII handling, and legal whistleblower exemptions.

1. The Anonymous Reporting Architecture

We recognize that members of the public who report serious immigration crimes, human trafficking rings, or corrupt employers may have legitimate safety concerns. ICE REPORT is engineered to allow completely anonymous participation:

  • No IP Address Logging: When you select anonymous submission, web server logs discard your IP address, device fingerprints, and browser user-agent signatures.
  • No Tracking Cookies: No persistent advertising, behavioral, or cross-site tracking cookies are stored on your device.
  • Automated Metadata Stripping: All image attachments (JPEG, PNG) and documents (PDF, DOCX) uploaded through the portal are automatically stripped of EXIF metadata, GPS geotags, and creator software timestamps prior to processing.
Unique Case Reference IDs for Anonymous Tipsters:

When submitting anonymously, you are issued a randomized reference code (e.g. ICE-TIP-2026-89412). This code allows you to append additional evidence or check status without ever exposing your real-world identity.

2. Data Encryption in Transit & at Rest

All communications between your web browser and the ICE REPORT platform are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3) protocols featuring AES-256 bit encryption and Perfect Forward Secrecy (PFS).

Data payloads routed to law enforcement intake systems are protected by FIPS 140-2 validated cryptographic modules, conforming to National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) Special Publication 800-53 standards for federal information systems.

3. Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) Exemption 7(D)

Under the Freedom of Information Act (5 U.S.C. § 552), federal records are generally subject to public disclosure upon request. However, tipster and confidential informant records are strictly protected under Exemption 7(D):

5 U.S.C. § 552(b)(7)(D) Protection:

Exempts records or information compiled for law enforcement purposes that could reasonably be expected to disclose the identity of a confidential source, including a state, local, or foreign agency or authority or any private institution which furnished information on a confidential basis.

This federal statutory shield guarantees that even if a suspect or defendant files a FOIA request, records disclosing the identity of confidential reporters remain permanently redacted and confidential.

4. Handling of Personally Identifiable Information (PII)

If you voluntarily choose to furnish your name, telephone number, or email address as a whistleblower or cooperating citizen:

  • Your PII is stored in restricted-access partitions accessible only to authorized federal criminal investigators.
  • Your contact details are never rented, sold, commercialized, or shared with commercial credit agencies or marketing organizations.
  • PII is retained strictly for the duration necessary to conduct the underlying investigation or subsequent judicial proceedings, after which records are archived in compliance with National Archives and Records Administration (NARA) General Records Schedules.

5. Digital Hygiene Tips for Sensitive Situations

If you are reporting from a shared home computer, an employer-owned workstation, or a public library terminal, follow these precautions:

  1. Use Incognito / Private Browsing: Always open your browser in private browsing mode before entering tips so local history is not cached.
  2. Use the Quick Exit Button: If someone approaches while you are on this site, click the red "Quick Exit" button in the top banner to immediately redirect the browser to the National Weather Service (weather.gov).
  3. Clear Local Storage & Cache: Clear your browser download history and delete any evidence photos stored on the local desktop once uploaded.
