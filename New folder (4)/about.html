About ICE REPORT

Dedicated to facilitating lawful civic participation, safeguarding national security, protecting vulnerable victims from exploitation, and preserving the integrity of U.S. immigration laws.

Our Platform Mission

ICE REPORT was established to provide United States citizens, lawful permanent residents, and community members with a streamlined, secure, and technologically advanced portal for reporting credible tips regarding federal immigration violations, human exploitation, and document fraud.

U.S. immigration laws exist to ensure national security, preserve fair economic opportunities for lawful workers, and safeguard human rights. Transnational criminal networks frequently abuse gaps in compliance to orchestrate dangerous human smuggling rings, operate coercive sweatshops, and conduct fraudulent schemes that jeopardize public trust. ICE REPORT bridges citizen vigilance with authorized law enforcement action.

Core Operating Principles

1. Fact-Based Objectivity

All submissions must rely on demonstrable, observable facts rather than speculation, rumors, or interpersonal animus.

2. Uncompromising Privacy

Citizens should never fear retaliation for fulfilling their civic duty. We offer rigorous anonymous reporting without digital tracking.

3. Humanitarian Vigilance

We prioritize tips involving modern slavery, forced labor, and child exploitation, providing immediate routing to crisis intervention specialists.

Statutory Integrity & Title 18 U.S.C. § 1001

The preservation of legal integrity requires that federal reporting channels remain unpolluted by vexatious, retaliatory, or fabricated claims. Under federal law:

18 U.S. Code § 1001 (Statements or entries generally):

Whoever, in any matter within the jurisdiction of the executive, legislative, or judicial branch of the Government of the United States, knowingly and willfully falsifies, conceals, or covers up by any trick, scheme, or device a material fact; makes any materially false, fictitious, or fraudulent statement or representation; or makes or uses any false writing or document knowing the same to contain any materially false, fictitious, or fraudulent statement or entry, shall be fined under this title, imprisoned not more than 5 years, or both.

Any user found deliberately leveraging this platform to harass neighbors, competitors, or spouses through knowingly fabricated allegations is subject to immediate referral to the U.S. Department of Justice for criminal prosecution.

Strict Non-Discrimination & Anti-Profiling Standard

ICE REPORT adheres strictly to the Department of Justice Guidance for Federal Law Enforcement Agencies regarding the elimination of racial profiling. It is unlawful and prohibited to report an individual solely on the basis of:

  • Race, ethnicity, skin color, or presumed ancestral origin
  • Accents or primary spoken language other than English
  • Religious attire, traditional grooming, or cultural customs
  • Lawful presence in public spaces, parks, or community gathering areas

Tips lacking specific factual indicators of unlawful conduct or criminal enterprise are rejected during preliminary screening.

Federal Disclaimer & Relationship to U.S. Government

ICE REPORT is a dedicated public service portal operating in strict alignment with federal reporting protocols established by the U.S. Department of Homeland Security (DHS) and U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE). While tips submitted through this gateway are packaged and routed according to authorized federal law enforcement intake guidelines, ICE REPORT is not a court of law and does not independently conduct arrests or physical field enforcement. All enforcement decisions rest exclusively with sworn federal and state law enforcement officers.
